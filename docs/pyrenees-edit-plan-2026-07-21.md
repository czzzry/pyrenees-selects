## Main Editorial Findings

1. Lock ranges and eliminate duplicates before stabilizing anything.
2. Many “extend” notes found materially better footage outside the original eight seconds. The strongest improvements are #9, #36, #39, #45, #46, #50, #63, and #79.
3. Candidate #19 improves after the original range because the mountain horizon appears, but it remains an alternate rather than a core shot.
4. Candidates #47, #74, and #75 cannot be extended: their source files are only 2.2, 3.0, and 3.0 seconds long.
5. Most smoothing requests are feasible with light Resolve stabilization plus trimming the abrupt end. Heavy stabilization should be avoided because it creates a visible crop.
6. Cropping boats or settlements is technically possible in 4K footage on a 1080p timeline, but cleaner alternative clips usually make a better editorial choice.
