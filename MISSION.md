# Mission: Review and Refine the Pyrenees Film in DaVinci Resolve

## Why
Learn the small set of Resolve skills needed to watch, evaluate, and refine the integrated drone-and-phone Pyrenees film without depending on someone else for every editorial adjustment.

## Success looks like
- Play, pause, scrub, and inspect the finishing timeline confidently
- Understand the source and timeline viewers
- Make and communicate precise timing, sound, and shot decisions
- Export a reviewed final film without altering the protected original timeline

## Constraints
- Learn through the real Pyrenees project, one practical operation at a time
- Prefer keyboard commands and plain-language explanations
- Preserve the original timeline while experimenting

## Out of scope
- Advanced Fusion compositing and professional color-grading theory
- Learning every Resolve page or command
