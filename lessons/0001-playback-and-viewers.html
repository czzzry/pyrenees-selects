Playback commands

CommandResult
SpacePlay or pause the active viewer
LPlay forward; tap again for faster playback
KStop

← →Move exactly one frame
⌘ + FToggle the selected viewer full-screen
One-minute practice
  1. Click the right-hand Timeline Viewer.
  2. Drag the red timeline playhead to the beginning.
  3. Press Space to play, then Space to pause.
  4. Use the arrow keys to inspect two individual frames.
  5. Press L, K, then J and notice the direction change.

Return to the Codex conversation and describe what each viewer showed. Ask whenever a control behaves differently.
